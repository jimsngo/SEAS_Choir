[Title]
Set Me On Fire

[Author]
St. Catherine of Sierra

[Verse 1]
Lord set me on fire with your love; a flame that nothing can contain. Burn every shadow deep within me. Let only your life remain.

[Refrain]
Set me on fire! A holy place inside my soul. Set me on fire! Let your love take full control every breath, every desire. Lord set my heart on fire!

[Verse 2]
Lord make me a vessel of your mercy, a beacon shining in the night. Fill me with courage for your mission, clothed in your perfect light.

[Bridge]
For when I burn with love for you. No fear can ever overcome. Your fire makes the weak grow strong. Your fire makes the broken whole.

[Refrain]
Set me on fire! A holy place inside my soul. Set me on fire! Let your love take full control every breath, every desire. Lord set my heart on fire!
