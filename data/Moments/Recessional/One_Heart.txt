[Title]
One Heart

[Author]
Curtis Stephan

[Verse 1]
You gather nations from east to west and every creature rightly sings your praise. For by the spirit you renew the earth, and give new life to all who cll you name. We celebrate the mighty victory for you rescued us from sin and grave. Blind us all together as one family, bind us all together.

[Refrain]
Make us of one heart and one mind, one body caught up in your love!
Let all creation in their elation rise up in praise, rise up to praise you!

[Verse 2]
To you we lift up our offering, one holy living sacrifice; pour forth your favor as we give you thanks and unite us all in Jesus Christ. Tear down the walls made by our gear and pride; set us free from every vanity. Blind us all together as one family, bind us all together.

[Refrain]
Make us of one heart and one mind, one body caught up in your love!
Let all creation in their elation rise up in praise, rise up to praise you!

[Bridge]
May the peoples praise you, God, from the rising to the setting sun, with no division among them. Let the earth resound with singing, everybody singing:

[Refrain]
Make us of one heart and one mind, one body caught up in your love!
Let all creation in their elation rise up in praise, rise up to praise you!
