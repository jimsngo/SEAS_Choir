[Title]
Sing to the Lord

[Author]
Jim Ngo xSuno

[Intro]
Sing to the Lord!
Sing a new song!
Sing to the Lord!
All of the earth!

[Verse 1 - Male]
Walking together down every street
Sharing his mercy with all we meet
Lifting each other when spirits fall
Showing his goodness out to them all

[Verse 2 - Female]
Opening hands to the broken and poor
Knocking with hope on every door
Living the gospel in all that we do
Making the old world beautiful, new

[Chorus - Call and Response]
(Full Choir) Sing to the Lord a new song!
Sing to the Lord, all the earth!
Tell of his glory all day long,
Praise him and sing of his worth!

[Bridge - Duet]
(Male & Female) Ascribe to the Lord all glory and strength
His love reaches out to the world's full length
Worship his name in the beauty of grace
Let every nation find their place

[Chorus - Call and Response]
(Full Choir) Sing to the Lord a new song!
Sing to the Lord, all the earth!
Tell of his glory all day long,
Praise him and sing of his worth!

[Outro]
(Full Choir) Sing a new song!
Sing a new song!
All the earth, sing out!
Sing to the Lord!
