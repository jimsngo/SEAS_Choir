[Title]
Dwell in Us - Advent 1

[Author]
Francesca LaRosa

[Antiphon]
To you, I lift up my soul, O my God.
In you, I have trusted; let me not be put to shame.
Nor let my enemies exult over me;
and let none who hope in you be put to shame.

[Verse]
Show us, Lord, your love; and grant us your salvation.
O come, Emmanuel, let us walk in the light of the Lord!
O come, Emmanuel, let us prepare the way of the Lord!

[Refrain]
Prince of Peace, King of Glory! Come;
Emmanuel, God with us.
Lord of Light, You are God's own Son,
dispel the darkness, dwell in us.
