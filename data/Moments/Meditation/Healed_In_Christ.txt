[Title]
Healed in Christ

[Author]Sarah Hart[Verse 1]
Father, you are here; holy, this anointing,gift of sweetest comfort in our suffering.God, we are afraid; you restore our courage.Though we are in darkness, bind our wounds with light.We are healed in Christ.

[Verse 2]
Jesus, you abide; gentle is your presence.Grant to us your strength, for we are weak of heart.In your loving cross is our restoration.Every scar we’ve carried, you have crucified.We are healed in Christ.[Refrain]Blessed be our God, healing us in Christ,consolation of our soul.Blessed be our God, healing us in Christ,making all that once was broken whole. (Repeat)[Verse 3]
Spirit, lead us home into our belonging.Love has made a way; our future is secured.And beyond the veil, all our tears forgotten,every last affliction we will leave behind;every shame forgiven as we find our life.And O how we shall sing forever in the skies!We are healed in Christ.
