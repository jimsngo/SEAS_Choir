[Title]
The Lord is my Shepherd 

[Author]
Josh Blakesley

[Refrain]
The Lord is my shepherd; there is nothing I shall want!
The Lord is my shepherd; there is nothing I shall want!

[Verse 1]
He lets me rest in open fields so green. He leads me to the quiet streams. He gives me strength and guides me in his ways. He renews his promises each day.

[Verse 2]
He is my guide, my shepherd and my King, and in my fear he offers peace. Though I walk through the valley, I will not be afraid. You will protect me; I'll be save.
