[Title]
We Come to You

[Author]
Josh Blakesley

[Verse 1]
By Your body and blood, by Your life and Your love; renew us.
May Your sacrifice be forgiveness in me; heal us.

[Refrain]
We come to You. We come to You broken, hungry.
We come to You. We come to You. Make us whole again.

[Verse 2]
In the body of Christ we are brought to new life; restore us.
May the flesh that we eat and the blood that we drink save us.

[Refrain - Repeat]

[Bridge]
By your love; by your grace; We are one.

[Refrain - Repeat]

[Verse 3]
By the faith we profess, and the sins we confess, mold us.
We are brought to our knees at the altar of peace; change us

[Verse 4]
May the Word we receive by the faith we believe; teach us.
With these mysteries draw us into the feast; feed us.

[Verse 5]
May the sacred prepare our hearts here laid bare; fill us.
By our lives we declare the Spirit we share; send us
