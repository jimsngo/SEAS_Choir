[Title]
A Voice Cries Out

[Author]Michael Joncas[Verse 1]
Console my people, the ones dear to me;speak to the heart of Jerusalem:the time of your mourning is ended now,the Lord of life will come.[Refrain]A voice cries out in the wilderness:"Prepare a way for the Lord!"A voice cries out in the wilderness:"Make straight a highway for God!"[Verse 2]
Ev'ry valley is made a plain, ev'ry mountain is leveled;the glory of God shall then be revealed,and the nations will sing in praise.[Verse 3]
A voice shouts: "Cry!" O what shall I cry?All flesh is like grass and its flowers:the grass may wither, the flowers may fade,but the Word of the Lord is forever.[Verse 4]
Zion, shout from the mountain top,lift up your voice, O Jerusalem,and say to the people of God's own land,"Behold, behold your God!"[Verse 5]
The Lord will appear as a shepherd,holding his lambs in his arms,keeping his flock so close to his heart,leading them all, old and young.
