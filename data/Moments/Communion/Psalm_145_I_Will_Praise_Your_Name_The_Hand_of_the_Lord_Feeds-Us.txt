[Title]
I Will Praise Your Name (Psalm 145)

[Author]
Scott Soper

[Refrain]
I will praise your name forever, 
my king and my God, my king and my God.

[Alternative Refrain]
The hand of the Lord feeds us; 
he answers all our needs, he answers all our needs.

[Verse 1]
I will praise you, God and king; 
I will bless your name forever. 
Every day I will bless you, 
praise your name forever.

[Verse 2]
You are grace and mercy, Lord, 
slow to anger, filled with kindness. 
You are good to all your children, 
loving all creation.

[Verse 3]
Let your works give thanks to you; 
let your faithful ever bless you; 
let them praise your strength, your glory, 
and proclaim your kingdom.

[Verse 4]
Every word you speak in faith, 
every work you do is holy. 
You lift up all who stumble, 
you raise up the lowly.
