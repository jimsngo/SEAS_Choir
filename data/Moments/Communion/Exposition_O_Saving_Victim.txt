[Title]
Exposition/O Saving Victim

[Author]Curtis Stephan/Steve Angrisano[Verse 1]
O King Exalted, savior of nations,we lift our eyes to heaven’s throne.Hear us, Redeemer, show us your mercy;we place our trust in you alone.[Refrain]O Saving Victim, open widethe gate of heaven to us below.Our foes press on from every side;thine aid supply, thy strength bestow.[Verse 2]
Might of the Father, way of salvation,no sin is hidden from your sight.Show us your mercy, free us from darkness,reveal to us your glorious light.
[Bridge]
Worthy is the Lamb.
